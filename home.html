THE 19TH CENTURY AS RIZAL'S CONTEXT

"THEN AND NOW"

SIMILARITIES

Similarities between the Philippines during the 19th century and today.

NO. TERMS / ASPECTS 19TH CENTURY TODAY
1 ECONOMY Agriculture and trade were important sources of livelihood. Agriculture, business, services, technology, and other industries provide sources of livelihood.
2 SOCIAL Families and communities played an important role in people's lives. Families and communities remain important parts of Filipino society.
3 CULTURAL Filipinos practiced traditions, customs, beliefs, and local celebrations. Filipinos continue to practice traditions and customs while adapting to modern influences.
4 POLITICAL / GOVERNANCE People lived under a system of government with political leaders and authorities. People continue to live under a government led by political leaders and public officials.
5 Daily Life Daily life involved farming, local trade, religious activities, and community interaction. Daily life involves work, school, technology, transportation, and social media.

DIFFERENCES

Differences between the Philippines during the 19th century and today.

NO. TERMS / ASPECTS 19TH CENTURY TODAY
1 ECONOMY The economy relied heavily on agriculture, farming, and colonial trade. The economy is more diverse, with services, technology, manufacturing, business, and international trade.
2 SOCIAL Society had a strict social hierarchy influenced by Spanish colonial rule. Society is more socially diverse, although differences in wealth and social status still exist.
3 CULTURAL Filipino culture was strongly influenced by Spanish traditions, Catholicism, and indigenous practices. Filipino culture combines indigenous traditions with Spanish, American, Asian, and other global influences.
4 POLITICAL / GOVERNANCE The Philippines was under Spanish colonial rule and had limited political independence. The Philippines is an independent democratic republic with elected government officials.
5 DAILY LIFE Daily life was slower and relied on farming, local trade, handwritten letters, and face-to-face communication. Daily life is faster and heavily influenced by technology, smartphones, the internet, social media, and modern transportation.
