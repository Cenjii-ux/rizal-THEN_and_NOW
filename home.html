THE 19TH CENTURY AS RIZAL'S CONTEXT

WRITTEN WORK ACTIVITY #2

"THEN AND NOW"

SIMILARITIES

Similarities between the Philippines during the 19th century and today.

NO. TERMS / ASPECTS 19TH CENTURY TODAY
1 ECONOMY Agriculture and trade were important sources of livelihood. Agriculture, business, services, technology, and other industries provide sources of livelihood.
2 SOCIAL Families and communities played an important role in people's lives. Families and communities remain important parts of Filipino society.
3 CULTURAL Filipinos practiced traditions, customs, beliefs, and local celebrations. Filipinos continue to practice traditions and customs while adapting to modern influences.
4 POLITICAL / GOVERNANCE People lived under a system of government with political leaders and authorities. People continue to live under a government led by political leaders and public officials.
5 Daily Life People communicated through letters, newspapers, and face-to-face interaction. People communicate through smartphones, social media, messaging apps, and the internet.

DIFFERENCES

Differences between the Philippines during the 19th century and today.

NO. TERMS / ASPECTS 19TH CENTURY TODAY
1 ECONOMY The economy relied heavily on agriculture and colonial trade. The economy is more diversified, including services, technology, manufacturing, and international business.
2 GOVERNMENT The Philippines was under Spanish colonial rule and did not have complete political independence. The Philippines is an independent democratic republic with elected leaders.
3 EDUCATION Access to formal education was limited, especially for many ordinary Filipinos. Education is much more accessible through public schools, private schools, colleges, and universities.
4 CULTURE Filipino culture was strongly influenced by Spanish colonial traditions and religion. Filipino culture combines local traditions with influences from different cultures around the world.
5 COMMUNICATION Communication was slower and depended on letters, printed materials, and direct interaction. Communication is much faster through smartphones, social media, email, and instant messaging.
